Where we're headed

Built as one shop, so you never need five.

Most print shops specialize in one thing — a sticker shop, a mug shop, an ID printer. East Prime runs sublimation, mug production, vinyl cutting, ID lamination, and DTF pressing under one roof, which means a school or office ordering an entire event's merch talks to one vendor instead of coordinating four.

No storefront means no overhead padding the price — and it means we're built from day one to work the way institutional buyers actually order: online, in bulk, on a schedule.
